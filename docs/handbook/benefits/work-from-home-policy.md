# Work from Home Policy

## Eligibility

- The benefit is unavailable during the first 6 months of service.
- The team lead may extend that waiting period.
- The employee must have stable power, internet backup, and a quiet home workspace.
- The company or team lead may revoke remote work temporarily or indefinitely because of work requirements or performance.
- HR controls category assignments; employees cannot change their own category.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source describes eligibility both as “after completion of six months with the company including the probation period” and as “not available during the first 6 months,” with a team lead able to extend the duration. It does not define the exact first eligible date or how an extended or 3-versus-6-month probation affects eligibility. State both claims and do not give a definitive eligibility date without HR confirmation.

## Category A: Lahore-based employees

Category A covers employees permanently living in Lahore. They must work from the office three working days each week:

- Monday and Wednesday: all Category A employees.
- Tuesday: Category A web-backend teams, including team leads.
- Thursday: Category A UI/UX and MERN teams, including team leads.
- Friday: Category A front-end, Marketing, and HR teams, including team leads.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** Team names and category mappings are volatile assignments, and the source's list of “current” team leads has no as-of date. Names are intentionally omitted here. Do not determine an employee’s office day from this schedule alone; verify the current category and mapping with HR.

## Category B: Outstation employees

Category B covers employees whose families live in other cities.

- Required office attendance: every working day for two consecutive weeks each month.
- Required weeks: the second and third weeks; they are fixed and cannot be changed.
- “Second and third week” means the second and third rows in the monthly calendar, not necessarily the second and third seven-day periods.

The source expects 100% team attendance on Mondays and Wednesdays during calendar rows two and three.

## Non-negotiable operating terms

### Power, internet, and availability

- Remote employees must maintain power and internet backup. Repeated outage-related unavailability may cause revocation of the benefit.
- Be consistently contactable from 10:00 AM–7:00 PM.
- Follow the standard hours, eligible-time, Hubstaff, break, and absence rules in [working hours and attendance](./working-hours-and-attendance.md).
- Wear acceptable attire for client or partner video conferences.

### Schedule adjustments

- Assigned days and number of days cannot be changed, split into half-days, or replaced with another personal arrangement.
- Emergency day swaps require team-lead approval by email with HR copied.
- After an approved swap, work from the office on the next scheduled work-from-home day.
- Maximum approved adjustments: 2 days per month.

### Late arrival and early departure on office days

- Standard office timing is 10:00 AM–7:00 PM.
- Arrival after 11:00 AM means the office day does not count and must be made up on the next work-from-home day.
- The source characterizes the expected office commitment as 10–12 working days per month and says no further adjustment or relaxation is provided except for emergencies.
- Do not leave early before completing that day’s working-hours requirement.
- In an emergency, inform the team lead before leaving early and make up the office day on the next work-from-home day.

### Travel

Outstation employees must arrange travel on weekends. Travel is not allowed during required office hours. If an employee travels then:

- the office day must be made up on the next work-from-home day; and
- leave must also be requested if working hours for that day are short.

### New team members

When a new team member joins, the team lead and the team’s members must be in the office. The source says they may compensate for late arrival by adjusting their work-from-home schedule.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The new-joiner rule does not define which team members must attend, what “late arrival” refers to, or how the adjustment is scheduled. Do not prescribe a specific make-up arrangement without team-lead and HR confirmation.

## Optional office attendance

The schedule defines minimum work-from-office attendance. An eligible employee may still work from the office on a day when remote work is allowed.

## Breaks

Lunch, Jumma, prayer, and logging rules are consolidated in [working hours and attendance](./working-hours-and-attendance.md).

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source specifies lunch and prayer times inside the work-from-home section but does not clearly state whether those breaks are paid or logged, or how they count toward daily eligible hours. Do not give a definitive answer; use the documented timings and ask HR to clarify time treatment.

## Commuter-benefit reference

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The [employee benefits policy](./employee-benefits.md) describes the remote-work schedule as providing “commuter benefits,” but this policy contains no commuter allowance, reimbursement, transportation, or separate commuter entitlement. Do not promise one without HR confirmation.

## Questions and category confirmation

Contact HR for the employee’s current category, office-day mapping, eligibility date, or any policy concern.
