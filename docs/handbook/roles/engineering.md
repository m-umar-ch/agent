# Engineering Role Ladder

Use this ladder with the [performance appraisal process](../performance-appraisal.md). It describes role scope and progression; assigned KPIs remain the basis for appraisal.

## Shared engineering expectations

Across the ladder, engineers are expected to:

- Participate in the software development life cycle at the level of ownership stated for their role.
- Understand requirements and contribute to architecture, design, and development of complex applications.
- Write clean, efficient, secure code using appropriate design patterns and coding standards.
- Participate in code review and uphold engineering standards and best practices.
- Collaborate with developers, designers, and other teams so applications meet business and user needs.
- Document application features, functionality, and guides.
- Participate in weekly calls and Scrum meetings.
- Debug issues and deliver projects on time.
- Keep role-relevant skills current, including Ruby on Rails, MERN, and frontend technologies, and complete training or courses needed for the role.
- From SE-I onward, write and maintain tests when needed and apply strong problem-solving skills to produce robust, optimized, scalable code.

## Associate Software Engineer (ASE)

- **Position:** Associate level
- **Department:** Engineering
- **Reports to:** Team Lead

### Responsibilities

- Participate in the full SDLC and in requirements understanding, architecture, design, and development.
- Implement work from provided specifications and participate in code reviews.
- Continuously learn and apply software-development best practices, especially for Ruby on Rails, MERN, and frontend work.
- Assist with documentation, collaborate across functions, resolve issues, and meet delivery timelines.
- Participate in the training program required to build Associate Software Engineer skills.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 0–6 months; fresh graduates may apply.
- **Preferred knowledge:** Ruby, Python, JavaScript, and React; Ruby on Rails, Django, Node.js, Next.js, and React frameworks.

## Software Engineer I (SE-I)

- **Position:** Junior level
- **Department:** Engineering
- **Reports to:** Team Lead

### Responsibilities

- Participate in the full SDLC and in requirements understanding, architecture, design, and development.
- Write and maintain tests when needed.
- Handle written communication under supervision: structure ideas clearly, observe calls and meetings, take detailed notes, and build an understanding of communication dynamics.
- Apply strong problem-solving skills to robust, optimized, scalable code.
- Perform the shared engineering expectations and complete learning needed for required skills.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 1 year in software development.

## Software Engineer II (SE-II)

- **Position:** Mid level
- **Department:** Engineering
- **Reports to:** Team Lead

### Responsibilities

- Independently execute the full SDLC.
- Lead requirements understanding, architecture, design, and development of complex applications.
- Independently handle written communication and lead calls and meetings, keeping discussion clear and guiding team participation.
- Assist the Team Lead with hiring assessments and interviews.
- Mentor and guide associate developers.
- Perform the shared engineering expectations, including testing as needed and timely delivery.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 2–3+ years in software development, including 1 year as SE-I.

## Senior Software Engineer I (SSE-I)

- **Position:** Senior level
- **Department:** Engineering
- **Reports to:** Team Lead

### Responsibilities

- Independently execute the full SDLC and lead requirements, architecture, design, and development.
- Lead written communication, calls, and meetings.
- Identify performance bottlenecks and improve backend and frontend infrastructure.
- Learn emerging technologies and provide technical direction and creative solutions to business problems.
- Mentor through peer programming and guide work regardless of complexity or scale.
- Support hiring and recruitment through assessments and interviews.
- Perform the shared engineering expectations, including tests as needed, code review, cross-functional delivery, and issue resolution.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 4+ years in software development.
- **Preferred:** AWS or related technology certification.

## Senior Software Engineer II (SSE-II)

- **Position:** Senior level
- **Department:** Engineering
- **Reports to:** Team Lead

### Responsibilities

The source assigns SSE-II the same delivery, technical leadership, communication, infrastructure improvement, emerging-technology, mentoring, and hiring responsibilities as SSE-I.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 5+ years in software development, including 1 year as SSE-I.
- **Required:** Hands-on experience independently planning and executing large-scale systems.
- **Preferred:** AWS or related technology certification.

## Principal Software Engineer

- **Department:** Engineering
- **Reports to:** Team Lead

### Responsibilities

- Independently execute the full SDLC.
- Drive company-wide architecture for complex systems, including technology stacks and design patterns, with scalability, performance, and maintainability in mind.
- Conduct code reviews, mentor engineers, and promote practices that grow the engineering team's skills.
- Write clean, efficient, secure code and maintain tests when needed.
- Track industry trends and emerging technologies, explore beyond frameworks, and introduce useful innovations into development.
- Collaborate across functions to meet business and user needs.
- Troubleshoot complex technical issues across the company.
- Help senior management formulate and implement development practices and policies.
- Collaborate with DevOps on deployment, monitoring, and maintenance of backend services.
- Identify technical debt and propose and implement remedies.
- Resolve backend and frontend performance bottlenecks.
- Offer technical direction and creative solutions to business problems.
- Participate in weekly calls and Scrum meetings and deliver projects on time.
- Mentor through peer programming and guide work regardless of complexity or scale.
- Support hiring and recruitment through assessments and interviews.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 7+ years in software development.
- **Preferred:** AWS or related technology certification.

## Team Lead responsibility

Team Lead is an additional responsibility, not a designation. It may be assigned to an engineer at SE-II or above based on demonstrated ability to lead and the criteria below.

- **Department:** Engineering
- **Reports to:** Head of Engineering

### Responsibilities

- Share broad knowledge of technologies and architectures and mentor the team.
- Collaborate with other leads and senior engineers to maintain high software standards.
- Promote company-wide coding standards, guidelines, and best practices, and participate in code reviews.
- Set challenging but realistic goals and guide the team toward successful delivery.
- Monitor team performance and encourage growth and development.
- Manage day-to-day project operations and scope.
- Hire high-performing engineers and continually raise the talent bar.
- Translate business KPIs for team members and track and evaluate their performance metrics and KPIs.
- Lead client communication for projects assigned to the team.
- Retain the responsibilities associated with senior and principal engineering scope.
- Mentor through peer programming and support recruitment through assessments and interviews.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 4+ years in software development.
- **Required designation:** SE-II or above.
- **Required evidence:** Strong collaboration with team members and participation in internal project communication and external client communication.
- **Required delivery experience:** Ability to manage multiple resources efficiently, with 3–4 years in technical service delivery.
- **Preferred:** Project management certification.
