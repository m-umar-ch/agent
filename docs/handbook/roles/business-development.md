# Business Development Role Ladder

## Scope of this guidance

These responsibilities are general guidelines and benchmarks, not fixed job descriptions. Final expectations are agreed individually at hiring, promotion, or role transition and may evolve with business needs and individual growth. Use them with the [performance appraisal process](../performance-appraisal.md) and assigned KPIs.

## Operating model

- **BDA:** Prepares lead data, supports early bidding, keeps CRM data organized, and supports SDRs and AEs.
- **SDR:** Owns top-of-funnel data, outreach infrastructure and execution, early qualification, and meeting booking.
- **AE:** Takes qualified opportunities through discovery, proposals, negotiation, and closing; the AE does not own list building or outbound operations.
- **Senior AE:** Owns strategic, high-value, multi-stakeholder opportunities and advanced negotiations.
- **BDM and above:** Manage people, systems, strategy, performance, and increasingly complex revenue decisions.

## Business Development Associate (BDA)

- **Position:** Associate level
- **Department:** Business Development
- **Reports to:** SDR, Account Executive, or BDM

### Responsibilities

**Cold email and LinkedIn**

- Collect and scrape lead data from LinkedIn with tools such as Skrap.io, Apollo, and Snov.io.
- Gather company and contact details from sources such as Lead411 and ZoomInfo.
- Clean names, roles, domains, and formatting for CRM import.
- Validate email lists with tools such as ZeroBounce and NeverBounce.
- Segment leads by ICP, industry, geography, and company size.
- Maintain organized lists for SDR sequences.
- Keep CRM statuses, fields, and attributes accurate.
- Help prepare outreach angles and personalization notes.
- Tag positive replies and hand them to an SDR or AE.

**Upwork, Fiverr, and other platforms**

- Scan jobs daily within selected ICP and niche categories.
- Draft structured proposals from templates with light personalization.
- Maintain profiles, portfolios, gig descriptions, and skills.
- Respond to early inquiries using pre-approved guidance.
- Track bids, responses, and follow-up schedules in CRM.

**Other**

- Participate in weekly BD meetings and pipeline reviews.
- Research competitors, niches, and trends.
- Complete training in communication, outreach, and bidding.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, or IT.
- **Required:** Strong research and documentation skills.
- **Preferred:** Experience with scraping tools such as Apollo and Snov.io.
- **Preferred:** Basic Upwork or Fiverr bidding experience.

## Sales Development Representative (SDR)

- **Position:** Junior level
- **Reports to:** BDM or Senior Account Executive

### Responsibilities

**Data and validation**

- Build refined lead lists using LinkedIn search, scraping tools, and data platforms.
- Validate emails through multiple steps with tools such as ZeroBounce and NeverBounce.
- Format, enrich, and segment CSV files accurately.

**Infrastructure and campaigns**

- Create and warm outbound email accounts.
- Monitor domain reputation, inbox health, and spam scores.
- Configure automated email and LinkedIn outreach sequences in CRM.
- Maintain CRM tags, workflows, lists, and automation rules.

**Outbound execution and optimization**

- Send daily sequences by ICP tier.
- Track delivery, bounce, and spam rates.
- Track opens, replies, clicks, and booking percentage.
- A/B test subject lines, templates, and sending times.
- Flag deliverability problems and escalate them to the BDM.

**Reply handling and qualification**

- Respond to warm replies quickly and professionally.
- Qualify leads using BANT or MEDDIC-lite criteria.
- Book introductory and discovery calls for AEs.
- Keep CRM notes, stages, and activity logs accurate.
- Follow up with unresponsive or partly interested prospects.

**Upwork, Fiverr, and other platforms**

- Submit personalized daily bids in predefined target niches.
- Respond promptly to platform messages.
- Gather requirements and prepare handover summaries.
- Draft proposal structures for AE review.
- Track bid success and adjust bidding angles with BDM guidance.

**Other**

- Report outreach metrics weekly.
- Propose new niches, keywords, and lead sources.
- Participate in BD training on objections, writing, and qualification.

### Qualifications

- **Required experience:** 1+ year in BD, SDR, or platform bidding.
- **Preferred:** Strong English writing and fast response capability.
- **Preferred:** Experience with CRM and outbound automation tools.

## BD Executive / Account Executive (AE)

- **Position:** Mid-level, full-cycle sales
- **Reports to:** BDM

### Responsibilities

**Qualified cold-email and LinkedIn opportunities**

- Take ownership of warm or positive SDR-qualified replies.
- Engage prospects professionally through email and LinkedIn.
- Conduct structured discovery covering technical and business needs.
- Document requirements and share them with engineering for estimation.
- Create proposals, pricing options, and statements of work.
- Present statements of work and development plans.
- Handle objections and negotiate scope, timeline, and price.
- Maintain pipeline hygiene and forecast accurately.
- Persistently follow up to move warm outbound leads toward closure.

**Upwork, Fiverr, and other platforms**

- Respond to high-potential leads with advanced messaging.
- Conduct voice or video discovery calls.
- Prepare custom proposals for the job and requirements.
- Convert one-time projects into long-term engagements or retainers.
- Consistently close small-to-mid-size deals.
- Maintain transparent communication and a strong client experience.

**Other**

- Coordinate closely with engineering for accurate estimates.
- Manage prospect relationships until handoff to delivery.
- Mentor BDAs or SDRs when needed.

### Qualifications

- **Required experience:** 2–3 years in BD with exposure to closing.
- **Preferred:** Strong negotiation and presentation skills.
- **Preferred:** Experience with US and EU clients.

## Senior BD Executive / Senior Account Executive

- **Position:** Senior full-cycle closer
- **Reports to:** BDM

### Responsibilities

**Strategic cold-email and LinkedIn opportunities**

- Own high-value, multi-stakeholder, and enterprise-level opportunities.
- Own strategic or high-value outbound leads.
- Lead deep technical, financial, and operational discovery.
- Engage mid-level and senior decision-makers.
- Create multi-phase proposals and detailed statements of work with engineering.
- Lead medium-to-large deal negotiations.
- Manage long sales cycles with persistent follow-up.
- Maintain accurate forecasts and CRM records.
- Convert warm prospects through relationship building.

**Upwork, Fiverr, and other platforms**

- Handle complex or high-budget platform deals.
- Create detailed, multi-stage statements of work with timelines.
- Lead advanced requirement analysis and technical scoping calls.
- Negotiate contract terms, pricing, and retainer structures.
- Train junior SDRs and BDAs in bidding practices.

**Other**

- Support the BDM on strategic deals and pricing reviews.
- Improve proposal templates and messaging frameworks.

### Qualifications

- **Required experience:** 4+ years in BD with a proven closing record.

## Business Development Manager (BDM)

- **Position:** Manager level
- **Reports to:** Senior BDM or Head of Business Development

### Responsibilities

- Lead the SDR and AE team and own its revenue targets.

**Outbound infrastructure and deliverability**

- Own email inbox and domain setup and warmup.
- Monitor domain reputation, inbox health, and spam indicators.
- Approve sequences, templates, and personalization angles.
- Review deliverability metrics and resolve issues.

**SDR oversight**

- Approve list building, scraping, and validation.
- Audit segmentation, ICP alignment, and list quality.
- Review reply handling and qualification notes.
- Monitor and improve SDR open, reply, and booking rates.

**Pipeline and sales**

- Maintain a clean SDR-to-AE handoff.
- Hold weekly pipeline reviews with AEs and Senior AEs.
- Maintain accurate forecasting across the BD unit.
- Support AEs in strategic discovery and negotiation calls.

**Upwork, Fiverr, and other platforms**

- Monitor bidding performance, message quality, and platform KPIs.
- Approve high-value proposals and guide statement-of-work structure.
- Join platform negotiations when needed.
- Maintain standards for profile quality, response time, and case studies.

**People and operations**

- Conduct weekly BD training and coaching.
- Coordinate with engineering for timely estimates.
- Lead SDR hiring and support AE hiring.
- Build and refine SOPs for outbound and platform workflows.

### Qualifications

- **Required experience:** 5–6+ years in BD.
- **Required:** Experience leading SDR and AE teams.

## Senior Business Development Manager (SBDM)

- **Position:** Senior management
- **Reports to:** Head of Business Development or CEO

### Responsibilities

- Own the efficiency, scalability, and health of the company-wide BD engine.

**Outbound strategy**

- Define quarterly and annual outbound strategy.
- Approve ICPs, verticals, and persona targeting.
- Align outbound work with Staunch's brand, positioning, and US, GCC, and EU markets.

**Infrastructure governance**

- Own domains, warmup tools, CRM automation, and outbound systems.
- Protect company-wide deliverability and sending reputation.
- Approve tools such as ZoomInfo, Apollo, Snov.io, and warmup services.
- Review the BDM's management of SDR operations.

**Performance and process**

- Audit open, reply, conversion, and pipeline metrics across teams.
- Maintain alignment from outbound through AE, BDM, and delivery.
- Improve messaging frameworks and qualification criteria.
- Support outbound playbooks and SOPs.

**Strategic deals and platforms**

- Participate in high-value negotiations.
- Approve major pricing decisions and multi-phase statements of work.
- Oversee the complete platform strategy.
- Approve enterprise-level platform proposals and deals.
- Govern niche selection, bidding, and proposal quality.
- Track platform KPIs at department level.

**Leadership**

- Build the annual BD hiring plan and KPIs.
- Develop compensation and incentive frameworks.
- Mentor BDMs, Senior AEs, and future BD leaders.
- Support the CEO with international expansion and major partnerships.

### Qualifications

- **Required experience:** 7–10+ years in BD with leadership responsibilities.

## Head of Business Development (HoBD)

- **Position:** Department head
- **Reports to:** CEO

### Responsibilities

- Lead the global BD function, drive revenue across channels, set strategic direction, and align BD with engineering, delivery, marketing, and company vision.
- Set annual revenue targets and outbound strategy.
- Approve new markets, ICP expansion, and global positioning.
- Ensure outbound quality meets brand and compliance standards.
- Oversee deliverability and outreach infrastructure.
- Validate department performance through quarterly reviews.
- Oversee enterprise platform deals and strategic platform direction.
- Approve retainer proposals and long-term contracts.
- Expand platform presence into new verticals and services.
- Represent Staunch at global events, conferences, and partnerships.
- Define BD hiring strategy, career paths, and organization structure.
- Collaborate with the CEO on company-wide strategy.
- Drive alignment across BD, engineering, delivery, and marketing.

### Qualifications

- **Required experience:** 10+ years in BD leadership.
- **Required:** Proven success scaling BD organizations and revenue pipelines.
