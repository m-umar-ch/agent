# Working Hours and Attendance

## Standard schedule and availability

- Full-time standard: 40 eligible working hours per week, normally 8 hours per day.
- The source also accepts 30–40 eligible hours per week, or 6–8 hours per day, depending on workload and work requirements.
- Fewer than 30 eligible hours per week, or fewer than 6 hours per day, is not acceptable.
- Office hours: 10:00 AM–7:00 PM.
- At least 5 working hours must be recorded during office hours; remaining eligible hours may be logged outside office hours.
- Employees must report on time, be ready to work on arrival, and return from breaks on time.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The employment classification defines a full-time employee by a standard 40-hour week, while this attendance section says 30–40 eligible hours may be acceptable depending on workload and work requirements and that fewer than 30 is unacceptable. A future agent must not treat 30 hours as a general full-time schedule or decide when fewer than 40 hours is approved without confirmation from the reporting manager or HR.

The [work-from-home policy](./work-from-home-policy.md) uses the same 10:00 AM–7:00 PM availability span and adds location-specific attendance requirements.

## Hubstaff logging and eligible hours

Hubstaff is the time-tracking and attendance system. It records work time, activity, and periodic screenshots.

An eligible work hour must have:

- significant Hubstaff activity of at least 20%; and
- activity relevant to assigned work, tasks, or learning.

Time generated only by cursor movement does not count. Time spent on lunch, prayer, social media, or other non-work activity does not count.

## Daily attendance from logged hours

If no leave is approved in Workable and recorded eligible hours are short:

- 0–3 hours logged: full-day absence.
- 3–6 hours logged: half-day absence.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source includes exactly 3 hours in both the “0–3” full-day range and the “3–6” half-day range, and does not state how 6 hours is classified. Do not give a definitive boundary answer for exactly 3 or 6 logged hours until HR confirms.

Hubstaff determines attendance automatically. Failure to meet time-logging requirements results in an absence unless the time is covered by leave marked and approved in Workable under the [leave policy](./leave-policy.md).

## Breaks

- Lunch entitlement: 1 hour.
- Source lunch schedule: Monday–Friday, 1:30 PM–2:30 PM.
- Friday Jumma break: 1:00 PM–1:30 PM.
- Namaz-e-Asr and Maghrib: at the employee’s discretion.
- Maximum additional time stated for prayers, tea or coffee, refreshments, and rest: 1 hour.
- Lunch, prayer, tea, and coffee time must not be entered as manual time and does not count as eligible logged work.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source grants a one-hour lunch break and separately sets fixed lunch, Jumma, and discretionary prayer times, but it does not say whether lunch or prayer breaks are paid, whether they must be logged in Hubstaff, whether the 8 eligible daily hours exclude them, or how Friday Jumma time interacts with the one-hour lunch and additional one-hour break cap. State these source claims and do not give a definitive paid-time or logging answer until HR confirms.

## Manual time

- Maximum accepted manual time: 6 hours per week; excess manual hours do not count.
- Do not add manual time for lunch, prayer, tea, or coffee breaks.
- Every manual entry in Hubstaff must include a comprehensive note explaining why it was entered.

## Tardiness and early departure

- If unable to report as scheduled, notify the reporting manager no later than the normal start time. Notification does not excuse tardiness.
- Before leaving earlier than scheduled, notify the reporting manager immediately.
- Office starts at 10:00 AM, with an emergency grace period through 11:00 AM.
- Arrival after 11:00 AM is late.
- Two late arrivals in one month equal one unpaid half-day leave.

Work-from-office days have stricter make-up rules; see [work-from-home policy](./work-from-home-policy.md).

## Absence deductions

An absence is a half or full day when an employee is not present and has not formally applied for and received leave approval in Workable.

Salary deduction formula:

`(monthly salary / number of days in that month) × number of days absent`

## Overtime and rewards

For an urgent delivery in a specific week, a team lead may approve extending the employee above 40 hours. Hours over 40 qualify for overtime only with team-lead approval.

Approval requires 40 or more productive hours on tasks assigned by the team lead. Learning, research, and other tasks are excluded from the overtime approval calculation.

The source calculates overtime in four-hour increments:

- 0–4 overtime hours: half day.
- 4–8 overtime hours: full day.
- 8–12 overtime hours: 1.5 days.

Per-day rate:

`monthly salary / number of days in that month`

Approved overtime is paid with the current month’s salary.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The overtime bands overlap at exactly 4 and 8 hours, and the source shows an ellipsis after the 8–12-hour example without defining higher bands or rounding. Do not give a definitive boundary, rounding, or above-12-hours answer until HR confirms.

## Weekends

Saturday and Sunday are off days. Team leads may call employees to work for urgent tasks or deadlines.
