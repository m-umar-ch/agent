# EOBI Policy

The Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution (EOBI) provides pension and income security for eligible private-sector employees under the Employees' Old-Age Benefits Act, 1976.

## Applicability and registration

- The EOBI scheme became effective on April 1, 1976.
- It applies to an establishment or industry that employed five or more people at any time during the preceding year.
- An employer must register its establishment with EOBI within 30 days after meeting the eligibility criteria.

## Employee eligibility

- Retirement age is 60 for men and 55 for women.
- Early-retirement or invalidity benefits may apply in some cases.
- The source policy states that, once registered, an employee becomes eligible for pension benefits upon reaching retirement age even if contributions do not continue until retirement.

## Contributions

- Total contribution: 6% of the government-declared minimum wage.
- Employer contribution: 5% of that minimum wage.
- Employee contribution: 1% of that minimum wage, deducted from salary.
- Contributions must be deposited by the 15th of each month.
- The government may optionally contribute under section 9A.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source calls PKR 37,000 the government-declared minimum wage “as of June 2025” and gives monthly amounts of PKR 1,850 from the employer and PKR 370 from the employee; its table also labels the employer amount “as per 2005 update.” These are dated source claims and government rates may change. Future answers must not present the minimum wage, contribution amounts, or their current legal status as definitive until HR confirms them.

## Benefits

EOBI assistance may include:

- old-age pension;
- invalidity pension;
- survivor's pension; and
- an old-age grant for a person who does not qualify for a monthly pension.

## Verify registration and contributions

1. Visit the [official EOBI website](http://www.eobi.gov.pk/).
2. Under **Facilitation System**, select **Personal Information**.
3. Select **Insured Person / Employee Details**.
4. Enter the employee's CNIC number to view status and total contributions.

## Claim a benefit

1. Visit the nearest EOBI regional office.
2. Submit the CNIC, EOBI registration number, and required evidence, such as proof of employment and age.
3. EOBI verifies contribution history and eligibility.
4. After approval, the pension is transferred to the nominated bank account.

Contact HR with questions about registration or benefits.
