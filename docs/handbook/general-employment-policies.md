# General Employment Policies

## Handbook Status and Policy Changes

- This handbook explains current policies and practices. It is not an employment contract, does not create an employment contract, and does not guarantee employment for any specific term.
- The handbook cannot address every workplace situation. Questions about how a policy or procedure applies should be directed to Human Resources (HR).
- Compliance with handbook policies is required. Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action.
- Staunch may change its policies and procedures at any time. The company will try to communicate changes by email or another appropriate channel, but notice is not required for a change to take effect.

## Equal Employment Opportunity

Staunch prohibits discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or marital status. This rule applies to:

- recruitment and hiring;
- training and development;
- promotion and advancement; and
- compensation and benefits.

## Employment Classifications

- **Full-time employee:** A permanent team member with a standard schedule of 40 hours per week.
- **Part-time employee:** A team member who works fewer hours than a full-time employee under an agreed schedule.
- **Contract employee or consultant:** A specialist engaged for a specific project or fixed period under the applicable contract.

## Recruitment, Onboarding, and Probation

Recruitment may include multiple interviews, a skills assessment, and a culture-fit evaluation. New employees receive onboarding on company processes and values.

The general employment section states that all new hires have a **3-month probation period**, during which Staunch evaluates performance, collaboration, and compatibility with company culture. Successful completion may lead to an offer of a permanent position.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The general employment policy sets probation at 3 months, but the leave policy refers to probation as "3 or 6 months." The source does not resolve which duration applies. A future agent must state both claims and must not give a definitive probation duration.

## Outside Work and Freelancing

Full-time employees must not:

- create, maintain, or work through a freelancing profile; or
- work for another company or client on a permanent or contractual basis.

A violation may result in disciplinary action, including termination.

## Personal and Business Relationships

Staunch prohibits:

- romantic or intimate relationships between current or former team members, regardless of role, seniority, or consent; and
- business relationships or partnerships with current or former team members, including co-founding a venture, commercial agreements, referral arrangements, and formal or informal business collaboration.

The source states that there are no exceptions. Report known violations directly to HR. A violation is subject to immediate disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Ignorance of the policy is not accepted as a defense.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The relationship policy calls for immediate disciplinary action, while the [offboarding policy](offboarding-policy.md#involuntary-termination) describes counseling followed by an investigation when counseling is unsuccessful. The source does not state which procedure governs a relationship-policy violation. A future agent must not predict the procedure or outcome.

## Confidentiality, Ownership, and Company Resources

### Protected information

- Treat project materials—including UI/UX designs, content, documents, and code—as highly confidential.
- Do not disclose project materials to a third party or use them in a personal project without explicit consent from Staunch and, when applicable, the client.
- Protect confidential information about company operations, clients, and employees from unauthorized disclosure.
- When transmitting confidential information, follow company-approved protocols, use secure channels, and comply with applicable encryption standards.

### Ownership

- Employees have no ownership claim over proprietary information or company-owned, developed, or purchased design, software, or hardware components.
- Proprietary information includes product applications, designs, technology, services, customer data, sales channels, trade secrets, and other confidential company information.
- Boilerplates, libraries, gems, and products developed by Staunch are company property. Do not use, share, or duplicate them without prior permission.
- Staunch may pursue legal remedies for a breach and seek recovery of litigation expenses and reasonable legal fees.

### Meetings and recordings

Internal meetings, discussions, and collaborations are confidential. Do not make audio or video recordings without the department head's explicit prior authorization. An authorized recording must be used only for official purposes and handled securely.

### Company resources

Use company laptops, software, internet access, and other resources responsibly for work-related purposes.

## Social Media and Public Sharing

- When sharing company achievements, milestones, or updates, do not disclose confidential or proprietary information.

## Communication Standards

### Internal and email communication

- Keep internal and external communication professional, concise, respectful, accurate, and appropriate.
- Use official email accounts and company-approved tools for work-related communication.
- Do not use personal email or unofficial platforms for sensitive or company-related correspondence.
- Avoid slang, overly casual language, and emoticons in formal communication. Check grammar and accuracy before sending.

### External communication

- Maintain a professional tone with clients, partners, and other external stakeholders.
- Escalate an external inquiry to your reporting manager when you are unsure how to respond.

## Complaints and Grievances

- Raise work-related concerns as soon as possible.
- Try to resolve a complaint informally and promptly where possible.
- Formal resolution or mediation may be used when informal resolution is not suitable or successful.
- Staunch states that complaints will be handled promptly, impartially, fairly, and under transparent procedures.
- Employees must not be penalized or disadvantaged for raising a complaint.
- Complaint information should be disclosed only to the involved parties.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source does not identify how or where to file a formal grievance, who owns each stage, the applicable response times, or how mediation is initiated. A future agent must not invent those procedural details.

## Disciplinary Grounds

Disciplinary action may result from:

- unsatisfactory work quality, performance, or attitude;
- excessive absenteeism or tardiness;
- misuse of breaks or lunch privileges;
- failure to follow procedures or safety rules;
- insubordination or damage to company property;
- a conflict-of-interest violation;
- unauthorized disclosure of confidential information;
- falsification of records or interference with another person's work;
- a criminal conviction that creates risk for the company or its employees.

The [offboarding policy](offboarding-policy.md) contains the separate discharge-for-cause grounds and the procedures for misconduct investigations, performance-related termination, and involuntary discharge.
