# 2. General Employment Policies

### **Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO)**
Staunch is committed to creating an inclusive workplace where every individual is respected and valued. As an equal opportunity employer, Staunch strictly prohibits discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or marital status.
This policy is applied across all stages of employment, including:
*   Recruitment and hiring
*   Training and development
*   Promotions and advancement
*   Compensation and benefits

### **Employment Types**
Staunch classifies employees into distinct categories, with terms and benefits tailored to each.
*   **Full-Time Employees**: Permanent team members working a standard schedule of 40 hours per week.
*   **Part-Time Employees**: Team members working fewer hours than full-time, with work expectations based on agreed schedules.
*   **Contractual Employees/Consultants**: Specialists hired for specific projects or on a time-bound basis, contributing to project-specific goals under contract terms.

### **Probation Period**
To ensure alignment with Staunch values and expectations, all new hires undergo a **three-month probation period**. During this time, performance, collaboration, and compatibility with company culture are closely evaluated.
*   **Probation Completion**: Employees who successfully complete probation may be offered a permanent position, reflecting a mutual commitment to their continued growth and success at Staunch.

### **Recruitment and Onboarding Process**
Our recruitment process is designed to be transparent and efficient. It includes a multi-stage interview process, skills assessment, and cultural fit evaluation. New employees will receive a comprehensive onboarding to understand our processes and values.

### **Freelancing/Working for a Third Party**
Full-time Staunch employees are not allowed to create, maintain, or work on any freelancing profiles. Additionally, employees are strictly prohibited from working for any other company or client on a permanent/contractual employment basis. Any violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment.

### **Workplace Relationship Policy**
Romantic or intimate relationships between current or former team members are not permitted at Staunch. This applies to all team members regardless of seniority, role, or whether the relationship is considered consensual. No exceptions will be made.
Business relationships and partnerships with current or former Staunch team members are strictly prohibited. This includes co-founding ventures, entering into commercial agreements, referral arrangements, or any form of business collaboration, whether formal or informal. No exceptions will be made.
Any team member found to be in violation of either of the above policies will be subject to immediate disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment. If you become aware of a violation, you are expected to report it to HR directly. Ignorance of this policy is not an accepted defense.

### **NDA / Design & Code Confidentiality**
All projects at Staunch—including created UI/UX designs, written content/documents, and code—are considered highly confidential. Employees are strictly prohibited from sharing this information with third parties or using it in personal projects without explicit consent from Staunch and, if applicable, the client.
Employees acknowledge that they have no claim of ownership or title over any proprietary information, including design, software, or hardware components owned/developed/purchased by Staunch. "Proprietary Information" encompasses all confidential, proprietary, and trade secret information related to Staunch’s products, including product applications, designs, technology, services, customer data, and sales channels.
All boilerplates, libraries, gems, and products developed by Staunch are the exclusive property of the company and cannot be used, shared, or duplicated without prior permission.
In the event of a breach of this agreement, Staunch reserves the right to seek legal remedies. In any litigation, the company is entitled to recover litigation expenses and reasonable attorney fees incurred in obtaining or defending such judgments.

#### **Use of Company Resources**
Company resources, including laptops, software, and internet, should be used responsibly for work-related purposes.

**Confidentiality Agreement**
At Staunch, we are committed to maintaining the trust and privacy of our clients, partners, and employees across the globe. As a service provider to international teams, we often handle sensitive and proprietary information, making confidentiality an essential aspect of our operat0ions.
All information, whether related to business operations, clients, or employees, must be treated as confidential and protected from unauthorized disclosure.
When transmitting confidential information via email, internal tools, the Internet, or other media, take all necessary precautions to safeguard it. This includes adhering to encryption standards, using secure communication channels, and following company-approved protocols.
Additionally, all internal meetings, discussions, and collaborations are considered confidential. You may not use audio or video recording devices to document meetings, conversations, or brainstorming sessions without the explicit prior authorization of your department head. Any such recordings, if authorized, must only be used for official purposes and handled securely.

### **Grievances Complaints:**
The purpose of this policy and procedure is to manage employees’ complaints, grievances, and problems effectively and efficiently. All attempts should be made to resolve the issue informally and as soon as practicable. The company will deal with grievances expeditiously, impartiality, and fairly.

# 2.1 Focus Hours Policy

To promote deep work and minimize distractions, the following Focus Hours Policy will be observed across the team:

### **Focus Hours Schedule**
*   **Time: 03:00 PM – 05:00 PM (every day)**
*   This time slot has been selected based on collective preference and will be strictly observed across all teams.

### **No Meeting Day**
*   **Thursday** is designated as a **No Meeting Day**.
*   All meetings must be scheduled outside of Thursday. Plan your week accordingly to respect this uninterrupted workday.

### **Mandatory Requirements During Focus Hours**
**Slack Status**
*       *   Update your Slack status to indicate that you are in Focus Mode (e.g., In Focus Hours – 03:00 PM to 05:00 PM 🚫).
    *   Enable “Do Not Disturb” mode during focus hours to prevent interruptions.
**Silent Work Environment**
*       *   No loud phone calls, Slack message tones, or notification sounds.
    *   All devices (laptops, phones, tablets, etc.) should be set to silent.
_This should be maintained throughout the day, not just during focus hours._
**Discussion Etiquette**
*       *   Strictly no **non-technical or non-project-related** discussions during focus hours.
    *   Technical discussions, if necessary, should be done quietly or via text-based communication (Slack etc.) without disrupting others.
**Respect for Others' Focus**
*       *   Avoid tapping colleagues, calling out across the room, or initiating casual conversations.
    *   If urgent communication is required, use Slack messages respectfully and expect responses after the focus period.

### **Compliance & Monitoring**
*   Team leads and reporting managers will help reinforce the policy.
*   Any consistent breach of these guidelines may be addressed in 1:1s or team retros.

  

Let’s all respect each other’s time and space during these hours so we can deliver our best work.

# 4. Workplace Safety & Ethics

### **Dress Code**
Employees are expected to dress professionally and smartly to promote and reflect the company’s image and values. Clothes that are typical in workouts and outdoor activities, shorts, and sleeveless shorts aren’t allowed unless specified. Avoid clothing with offensive or inappropriate prints.

# 6. Performance Evaluation & Appraisal Process

#### **Step 01: Initial Meeting with Team Member(s)**
*   Team leads will initiate the process by meeting with their respective team members.
*   This collaborative effort aims to refine **KPIs**, set clear performance expectations, and establish a solid foundation for the appraisal process.
#### **Step 02: Team Member Responsibilities**
*   **Regular Updates -** Each team member must update their **appraisal boards** specifically every month (manadatory). These updates should include progress on assigned KPIs, completed tasks, and any relevant achievements to ensure accurate performance tracking.
#### **Step 03: Team Lead Review**
*   **Quarterly Review -** Team leads will review their team members’ appraisal boards at the end of each quarter. This ensures alignment with organizational goals and provides actionable feedback.
*   **Q1 Deadline**: Last week of September.
*   **Q2 Deadline**: Last week of December.
*   **Q3 Deadline**: Last week of March.
*   **Q4 Deadline**: Last week of June.

**Optional Monthly Reviews**:
*   While quarterly reviews are mandatory, team leads may conduct optional monthly reviews for more frequent feedback and support.
#### **Step 04: Rating and Comments**
*   After each review period, team leads will **assign ratings** to their team members based on the performance recorded on the appraisal boards.
*   **Mandatory Comments -** Team leads must provide detailed comments explaining their ratings. These comments will serve as a valuable resource for understanding strengths, identifying areas for improvement, and outlining actionable next steps.
### **Employee Annual Appraisal**
*   Each employee’s performance will be formally **appraised annually**, with salary increments determined based on their performance.
*   The **increment appraisal** will occur **exactly 12 months** from the employee’s last salary increment.
*   Increments will be directly linked to **KPIs**, which will be shared individually with each employee through the ClickUp board.

#### **Why This Process Matters?**
Fostering a transparent and regular feedback culture is essential for individual and team growth. Your active participation and commitment to this process are crucial for its success.
By following this process, we aim to empower our employees, recognize their contributions, and provide the necessary support to help them achieve their career aspirations within Staunch. 🚀

#### **Where can I find KPIs for myself?**
The KPIs for each team working at Staunch are very well defined and will be shared with the team members and their team leads via Clickup. These KPIs are divided into 3 categories: Functional area key responsibilites, learning and general factors.

# 7.4 EOBI Policy (Employees’ Old-Age Benefits Institution)

### **What is EOBI?**
The Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution (EOBI) is a federal statutory body that provides **pension** and **income security** to employees of private sector establishments upon retirement, disability, or death, under the EOBI Act, 1976.

### **Legal Basis & Applicability**
*   EOBI became effective on April 1, 1976.
*   It applies to all establishments or industries that have employed five or more persons at any time during the preceding year.

### **Who is Eligible?**
*   **Men:** Upon reaching **60 years of age**
*   **Women:** Upon reaching **55 years of age**
*   In some cases, early retirement or disability benefits may apply.

### **Employer Registration**
*   Employers are legally required to register their establishments with EOBI within 30 days of meeting the eligibility criteria.

### **Contribution Structure**
*   The current contribution rate is 6% of the government-declared minimum wage (Rs 37,000 as of June, 2025, subject to change as per Govt. policies):
    *   5% (of the minimum wage) paid by the employer
    *   1% (of the minimum wage) deducted from the employee’s salary
*   Contributions must be deposited by the 15th of every month.

| **Who Pays?** | **Contribution Amount** | **Rate** |
| ---| ---| --- |
| **Employer** | Rs: 1850 | 5% of the employee’s minimum wage (as per 2005 update) |
| **Employee** | Rs 370 | 1% of the wage |
| **Govt.**<br>**(optional)** |  | May contribute under section 9A |

### **Benefits for Employees**
*   Once registered, employees become eligible for pension benefits after reaching the retirement age, regardless of whether they continue contributing until retirement.
*   EOBI provides financial assistance in the form of old-age pension, invalidity pension, survivor’s pension, and old-age grant (for those who do not qualify for the monthly pension).

### **How to Verify Your EOBI Status**
Visit the official EOBI website: [www.eobi.gov.pk](http://www.eobi.gov.pk/)
**→** Under the Facilitation System tab, click on **“Personal Information.”**
→ Select **“Insured Person / Employee Details”**
→ Enter your **CNIC number** to view your total contributions and status

### **How to Avail**
1. Visit your nearest EOBI regional office.
2. Submit your CNIC, EOBI registration number, and required documents (e.g., employment proof, age verification).
3. EOBI will verify your contributions and eligibility.
4. Once approved, your pension will be transferred to your nominated bank account.

# 7.5 Staunch Night Work Compensation Policy

Staunch is pleased to announce a nighttime working policy to ensure the continued delivery of work for our clients and fair compensation for team members' efforts.

*   **Effective Date:** This policy is effective starting **July 17, 2026**.

### **Definitions**
**Office Hours:** 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM
**Night Hours:** 9:00 PM – 2:00 AM
**Eligible Projects:** Based on the client's needs, workload, and previous data, management will determine which projects are eligible for night-work compensation. The list will be revised every month to ensure it remains up to date with ongoing needs.
**Standby Duty:** A scheduled period during Night Hours in which an employee is required to remain reachable and responsive, regardless of whether work is assigned during that period.
**Standby Duty Roster:** For each project, a standby duty roster will be maintained to clarify which members are available on Standby for that day. Each member must be available for 2 days and 3 days on alternate weeks.
**Night Work:** This includes urgent bugs or issues encountered in any project, go-live support, and urgent features.

### **Classification of Eligible Projects**

*   **Tier 1 Projects:**
This tier will include high-priority projects of strategic importance for the company. These are the ones where response time matters. And hence the need for a standby duty roster. The factors to add any project to this tier are:
*   Important projects about to be launched
*   Recently launched projects
*   Projects of very high usage and high support requirements
*   Projects where urgent features are needed.

*   **Tier 2 Projects:**
This tier will include medium-priority projects. These are the ones where a standby duty roster is not needed, and team members can be called on as needed. The factors to add any project to this tier are:
*   Launched project with growing usage
*   Ongoing steady development
*   Occasional need to work on any support tasks or bug fixes.

All other projects (MVP development, enhancements work in launched and stable projects, etc) are not entitled for any night work benefits. All the work on these should be done during standard office hours. Overtime will be paid for these projects only, in accordance with the overtime policy.

### **Components of Compensation**

*   **Standby Allowance**
*   Paid to employees formally rostered for Standby Duty on a given month.
*   Rate: A fixed allowance of PKR 30,000 per month for all team members.
*   Paid regardless of whether the employee is actually engaged for work during the standby period.
*   Applies only to employees on the official Standby Roster. Employees working late by personal choice or habit, without being rostered, are not eligible for this allowance.

*   **Night Work Pay**
*   Paid for actual hours worked during Night Hours, as recorded via Hubstaff.
*   Rate: Your standard hourly rate x 1.5 (standard hourly rate = your total monthly salary / 160)
*   Applies only to employees on the Standby Roster.
*   A monthly threshold of 5 hours of incidental Night Work is not compensated; hours beyond the threshold are paid in full.
*   Additionally, there is a cap of 30 hours per team member per month. Team leads are required to get additional hours approved via email to HOE, with HR CC'd.
*   Standby employees who are engaged for actual work during their standby window receive both the Standby Allowance (3.1) and Night Work Pay (3.2) for hours worked.

# 8. Off Boarding

Offboarding is a discrete and important process. But it is also part of a larger picture—the employee lifecycle. This spans from long before an employee’s first day until long after the employee departs.
Effective onboarding, performance management, and off boarding processes shows that Staunch understands an effective employer-employee relationship. 
At Staunch, we recognize that offboarding procedures vary depending on the type of termination—whether resignation, layoff, or the completion of a contract. Our goal is to ensure a seamless transition, leaving no loose ends when an employee departs. Additionally, the offboarding process provides valuable insights into areas where we can enhance the experience for both current and future employees. We are committed to handling all terminations—whether voluntary or involuntary—with professionalism and minimal disruption to the workplace. Below is a clear outline of the procedures and expectations regarding employee resignations, discharges, and related processes.
## **Voluntary Termination**
**Resignation**
Employees intending to resign are expected to notify their direct manager and the HR department in writing. This notification should include:
*   Reason for leaving
*   Last working day
Employees must submit this written resignation at least **one month** before their intended last day of work. This allows the company adequate time to manage the remaining workload and transition responsibilities. The HR department will coordinate the resignation process, which includes:
*   Returning all company property
*   Reviewing post-termination benefits
*   Completing an exit interview
**Failure to Show Without Notice**
If an employee fails to show up for work for a specified number of days without notice, it will be treated as a voluntary resignation.

## **Involuntary Termination**
**Discharge for Cause**
In some cases, an employee may be terminated immediately due to misconduct or severe violations of company policies. This may include, but is not limited to:
*   Breaching the employment contract
*   Fraud, embezzlement, or illegal activities against the company
*   Discriminatory or harassing behavior
*   Unlawful or immoral conduct on the job
*   Willful neglect of job responsibilities
*   Intentional damage to company assets
*   Continued disregard for company policies

*   **Discharge Without Cause**
Employees may also be discharged without cause, such as during layoffs or due to poor or unsatisfactory performance, including during probation periods.
In cases of misconduct or serious misconduct, Staunch will first attempt to address the situation through counseling or other appropriate actions. If these efforts are unsuccessful, an investigation will be initiated to determine if the issue is substantiated. The process will be fair, objective, and transparent.
If misconduct is confirmed, disciplinary actions may include suspension with or without pay, or termination of employment.

*   **Discharge Process**
Before proceeding with an involuntary discharge, the reporting manager must request a review by the **Termination Review Board**, which includes the CEO, department head, an HR representative, and the team lead. This board will review the situation and determine whether discharge is warranted. If the recommendation is for discharge, the HR representative and the reporting manager will notify the employee.

*   **Termination During the Probationary Period**
If an employee’s performance during the probationary period is deemed unsatisfactory, the company reserves the right to terminate employment, subject to providing the required notice.

*   **Termination Due to Unsatisfactory Performance**
If performance issues arise, the reporting manager will first counsel the employee and provide clear expectations for improvement. The manager will also offer measurable strategies for performance enhancement and support resources.
The employee will be given a written notice outlining:
*   The areas of performance needing improvement
*   The proposed date for a performance review
*   Available resources to assist in improving performance
The employee will have **seven working days** from receipt of this notice to respond.

*   **Formal Report and Disciplinary Action**
If performance does not improve after counseling, the reporting manager will submit a formal report to the HR department, detailing the unsatisfactory performance and prior attempts to resolve the issue. The manager will also share this report with the employee.
The HR department and reporting manager will review the report and decide on the next steps, which may include:
*   No further action
*   Disciplinary action, such as:
*   Suspension with or without pay
*   Termination of employment
Any disciplinary action will take effect no earlier than **five working days** after the written notice is provided to the employee.

*   **Return of Company Property**
Upon resignation, discharge, or termination, employees must return all company property, including laptops, mobile phones, keys, and identification cards. Failure to return company property may result in deductions from the final paycheck, where permitted by law.

*   **Exit Interview**
Employees leaving Staunch, regardless of the reason, must attend an exit interview with a member of the HR team. This interview typically takes place during the final week of employment. The HR department will also ensure the employee completes a clearance process to confirm the return of all company property.

*   **Eligibility for Rehire**
Employees who leave Staunch in good standing, with proper notice, may be eligible for rehire. Former employees must apply through the regular application process and meet all the minimum qualifications for the position, including any required assessment.
