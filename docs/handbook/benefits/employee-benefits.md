# Employee Benefits

## Scope and administration

HR administers the benefits programs and is the contact for questions, coverage checks, and claims assistance. Staunch may modify, amend, or end a benefit program at any time; significant changes are communicated by email and at town halls.

## Payroll and USD salary component

- Payroll is processed on the 4th of each month and paid monthly by direct bank transfer.
- The USD salary component applies when monthly salary exceeds PKR 200,000.
- The source example pays a PKR 250,000 salary as PKR 200,000 plus the remaining PKR 50,000 in USD.
- Employees receiving the USD component must set up a Payoneer account.
- [Source tax chart for 2024–25](https://t7249714.p.clickup-attachments.com/t7249714/8e6f6cc4-0a62-4bd4-a713-e64d7024c299/image.png)

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The PKR 200,000 threshold, salary split example, and linked tax chart are explicitly tied to the 2024–25 tax year. Do not present them as current tax guidance or a current payroll entitlement until HR confirms the applicable tax year and values.

## Medical benefits

### In-patient insurance

- Eligibility: full-time employees after successful completion of probation.
- Provider named in the source: Smart Benefits (Jubilee).
- Covered services listed in the source:
  - pre- and post-hospitalization charges;
  - hospitalization and in-patient care;
  - accidental enhancement;
  - accidental emergency services;
  - medical emergencies; and
  - special investigation tests, including CT scans, MRI, and endoscopy.
- Annual coverage per person: PKR 400,000.
- Daily hospital room limit: PKR 12,000.
- Chughtai Lab discount: 20%.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The provider, annual coverage, room limit, lab discount, and covered services are plan-specific values in the source. Verify the employee's current plan and limits with HR before presenting these amounts or services as current coverage.

For hospitalization or a medical emergency, use the health card at a panel hospital. Each employee receives a portal for plan, coverage, and claim-status information. Contact HR before relying on a service being covered.

[Medical insurance reference slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1ZGwexXMULqB8KeLXdMxek_-xQW_7yXGG/edit?usp=drive_link&ouid=102636111099455209830&rtpof=true&sd=true)

### Outpatient allowance

- Eligibility stated in the source: permanent full-time employees from their date of hire.
- Allowance: 10% of basic salary, described in the source as tax-exempt.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source says in-patient medical insurance starts after probation, but says permanent full-time employees receive OPD coverage from the date of hire. It also defines probation as three months in the general employment section and “3 or 6 months” in the leave section. Do not give a definitive medical start date, probation duration, or current tax-treatment answer until HR confirms how these statements apply together.

## Learning assistance

- Staunch arranges training sessions and professional-development workshops.
- An external program completed by an employee is eligible for 100% reimbursement when approved by the employee’s team lead.

## Mobile allowance

The source provides a mobile allowance to Marketing and HR team members who need to contact external vendors for company purposes.

## Company laptop

Full-time permanent employees receive a company laptop after completing probation.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** Laptop eligibility depends on probation completion, while the source describes probation as both three months and “3 or 6 months.” Do not state a definitive eligibility date without HR confirmation.

## Long-service awards

- Eligibility: full-time employees reaching 3, 5, 7, or 10 years of continuous service.
- Amount: two times the higher of the employee’s monthly USD salary component or monthly PKR salary component in the fiscal year when the milestone is completed.
- Timing: processed at the end of that fiscal year and paid with the second salary of the next fiscal year.
- Currency: paid in USD unless the employee requests PKR; the employee bears tax on a PKR award.

## Company-sponsored recreational trip

Staunch sponsors one recreational trip annually and covers the trip expenses.

## Workplace support commitments

The company-values section lists:

- additional leave for employees of other religions where needed;
- a daycare facility; and
- a female prayer area, female lounge, and dedicated female restrooms.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** These items appear as diversity commitments rather than detailed benefit policies. The source does not define eligibility, availability, locations, request procedures, or the amount of additional religious leave. A future agent may identify the documented commitments but must not promise a specific facility or leave entitlement without HR confirmation.

## Work-life balance and commuter benefit

Flexible scheduling and remote-work options are offered under the [work-from-home policy](./work-from-home-policy.md).

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The benefits section calls the designated remote-work schedule a “commuter benefit,” but the work-from-home policy specifies no commuter allowance, reimbursement, transport, or other separate commuter benefit. Do not promise a monetary or transport benefit; refer only to the documented remote-work schedule until HR clarifies.

## Interest-free loan

### Eligibility and limits

- Loans are considered case by case; performance, history, and loyalty are stated approval factors.
- Minimum service: 2 years (24 months).
- Maximum amount: three times total monthly salary, combining PKR and USD components.
- Interest and processing fees: none.
- Repayment: payroll deductions over no more than 12 months from disbursement.
- Capacity: a maximum of five applications per annum are entertained, first come, first served. The source does not specify whether this cap is company-wide or per employee.
- A new loan may be requested at least 6 months after the previous loan is paid off.
- An employee using one loan facility cannot apply for another “loan/benefit” at the same time.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** One source line leaves the maximum repayment term as the literal placeholder “Loan payback period,” while the next sentence explicitly caps payroll repayment at 12 months from disbursement. Retain the 12-month maximum, but do not provide a more specific installment schedule. HR must also clarify the scope of the five-applications-per-year cap and what “another loan/benefit” includes.

### Application and approval

1. Complete the [loan application form](https://docs.google.com/document/d/18YBm2z2tYAhdLCEeNb8DM8hlJa_ro4X9/edit?usp=drive_link) and submit its required documents.
2. Provide a personally signed cheque, made out in the borrower’s own name, as a guarantee.
3. The source says examination and approval generally take two weeks from the request date.

### Repayment, default, and closure

- Late-payment fees and default consequences may apply, but the source gives no amounts or rules.
- A designated dispute-resolution process is referenced but not defined.
- The company may terminate the loan facility in unspecified circumstances.
- On full repayment, the loan is closed, the previous loan letter is cancelled, and the guarantee cheque is returned.
- If the employee resigns or employment is terminated during repayment, all outstanding loan amounts must be cleared immediately.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** Late fees, default consequences, dispute steps, and grounds for terminating the facility are incomplete in the source. Do not quote amounts, timelines, or outcomes beyond the explicit terms above.
