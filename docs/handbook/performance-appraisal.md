# Performance Appraisal

## Roles and Systems

- **Team lead:** Initiates KPI alignment, reviews appraisal boards, assigns ratings, and provides comments.
- **Team member:** Keeps the appraisal board current with KPI progress, completed work, and relevant achievements.
- **System of record:** ClickUp hosts appraisal boards and individually shared KPIs.

## Initial KPI Alignment

The team lead meets with each team member to refine KPIs, set performance expectations, and establish the basis for appraisal.

KPIs are grouped into three categories:

1. functional-area key responsibilities;
2. learning; and
3. general factors.

## Monthly Team-Member Updates

Each team member must update their appraisal board **every month**. Each update should record:

- progress against assigned KPIs;
- completed tasks; and
- relevant achievements.

## Quarterly Team-Lead Reviews

Team leads must review each team member's appraisal board at the end of every quarter.

- **Q1:** Last week of September
- **Q2:** Last week of December
- **Q3:** Last week of March
- **Q4:** Last week of June

Team leads may also conduct optional monthly reviews for more frequent feedback and support.

## Ratings and Comments

After each review period:

1. the team lead assigns a rating based on the performance recorded in the appraisal board; and
2. the team lead adds detailed comments explaining the rating, strengths, improvement areas, and actionable next steps.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source does not define the rating scale, evidence standards, calibration process, approval authority, or a process for employees to question or appeal a rating.

## Annual Appraisal and Salary Increment

- Each employee receives a formal annual performance appraisal.
- Salary increments are determined based on performance and linked directly to KPIs.
- An increment appraisal occurs **exactly 12 months after the employee's last salary increment**.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source does not define the starting date for an employee who has not previously received a salary increment, nor does it state when an approved increment takes effect or is paid.
