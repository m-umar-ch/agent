# UI/UX Role Ladder

Use this ladder with the [performance appraisal process](../performance-appraisal.md). Assigned KPIs remain the basis for appraisal.

## Shared design expectations

From UI/UX Designer I through Senior UI/UX Designer II, recurring expectations are to:

- Create user-centered designs from business requirements and user feedback.
- Translate requirements into style guides, design systems, design patterns, and effective interfaces.
- Design interface controls, navigation, and informational components.
- Identify and resolve UX issues such as responsiveness.
- Maintain design consistency and contribute to guidelines and standards.
- Build robust, flexible design systems covering typography, color, and components.
- Stay current with UI/UX trends and design skills and deliver projects on time.

Level-specific sections below add the ownership, research, communication, handoff, leadership, and qualification expectations that distinguish progression.

## Associate UI/UX Designer

- **Position:** Associate
- **Department:** Design
- **Reports to:** Design Lead

### Responsibilities

- Assist with user research and gather feedback through interviews, surveys, and usability testing.
- Support competitive analysis, UX audits, and related documentation.
- Create wireframes and low-fidelity mockups.
- Support interface design with attention to aesthetics, usability, and brand consistency.
- Collaborate with senior designers to refine interactions and flows.
- Work with product managers, developers, and designers to understand requirements and deliver solutions.
- Participate in brainstorming and contribute ideas that improve the user experience.
- Document design processes, user flows, and project materials.
- Assist with high-fidelity prototypes in tools such as Figma, Adobe XD, or Sketch.
- Follow design systems and brand guidelines.
- Apply grids, layouts, typography, and color effectively.
- Produce pixel-accurate, developer-ready designs and review implementations against the intended design.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 0–1 year.
- **Required behaviors:** A positive learning attitude; openness to challenges, constructive critique, iteration, and improvement from user and team feedback.
- **Required knowledge:** Current UI/UX trends, tools, and best practices.
- **Preferred:** UI/UX- or design-related degree or certification.

## UI/UX Designer I

- **Position:** Junior level
- **Department:** Design
- **Reports to:** Design Lead (Team Lead)

### Responsibilities

- Perform the shared design expectations.
- Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and mockups.
- Collaborate with product, engineering, and management.
- Incorporate client and user feedback, usage metrics, and usability findings to improve the experience.
- Continually improve accessibility, UX research, and design processes.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 1–2+ years.
- **Preferred:** UI/UX- or design-related degree or certification.

## UI/UX Designer II

- **Position:** Mid level
- **Department:** Design
- **Reports to:** Design Lead (Team Lead)

### Responsibilities

- Perform the shared design expectations.
- Lead user research, including interviews, surveys, and usability testing.
- Create and refine user personas, journey maps, and user flows.
- Communicate proficiently and lead client calls.
- Work closely with business development, product management, and development teams.
- Explain and defend design choices to technical and non-technical audiences.
- Participate constructively in design critiques.
- Provide assets and specifications that enable developers to implement designs accurately.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 2–3+ years, including 1 year as UI/UX Designer I.
- **Preferred:** UI/UX- or design-related degree or certification.

## Senior UI/UX Designer I

- **Position:** Senior level
- **Department:** Design
- **Reports to:** Design Lead (Team Lead)

### Responsibilities

- Perform the shared design expectations.
- Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and mockups.
- Collaborate with product, engineering, and management.
- Incorporate client and user feedback, usage metrics, and usability findings.
- Provide assets and specifications for accurate developer implementation.
- Continually improve accessibility, UX research, and design processes.
- Lead, mentor, and scale a team capable of high-quality UI/UX and graphic design work.
- Demonstrate high-quality practice across research, ideation, strategy, interaction design, and visual design.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 4+ years.
- **Preferred:** UI/UX- or design-related degree or certification.

## Senior UI/UX Designer II

- **Position:** Senior level
- **Department:** Design
- **Reports to:** Design Lead (Team Lead)

### Responsibilities

The source assigns Senior UI/UX Designer II the same user-centered design, prototyping, collaboration, feedback integration, design-system, developer-handoff, accessibility, team leadership, and end-to-end design-practice responsibilities as Senior UI/UX Designer I.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 5+ years, including 1 year as Senior UI/UX Designer I.
- **Preferred:** UI/UX- or design-related degree or certification.

## Principal UI/UX Designer

- **Department:** Design
- **Reports to:** Design Lead (Team Lead)

### Responsibilities

- Define and drive the overall UI/UX vision and strategy in alignment with business objectives and user feedback.
- Collaborate with product, engineering, and management.
- Define personas, journeys, and use cases that inform design decisions.
- Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and mockups.
- Translate requirements into style guides, design systems, patterns, and interfaces.
- Design interface controls, navigation, and informational components.
- Identify and resolve UX issues such as responsiveness.
- Incorporate client and user feedback, usage metrics, and usability findings.
- Maintain design consistency and develop guidelines and standards.
- Provide assets and specifications for accurate implementation.
- Build flexible design systems covering typography, color, and components.
- Improve accessibility, UX research, and design processes.
- Lead, mentor, and scale a team capable of high-quality UI/UX and graphic design work.
- Demonstrate high-quality practice across research, ideation, strategy, interaction design, and visual design.
- Deliver projects on time.
- Align visual and interactive design with brand identity in partnership with marketing and branding.
- Experiment with new design approaches to improve engagement and experience.
- Stay current with UI/UX trends and design advances.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 6+ years.
- **Preferred:** UI/UX- or design-related degree or certification.

## Team Lead responsibility

Team Lead is an additional responsibility, not a designation. It may be assigned to a designer at UI/UX Designer II or above based on demonstrated leadership ability and the criteria below.

- **Department:** Design
- **Reports to:** Head of Engineering

### Responsibilities

- Lead design reviews and resolution of UI/UX issues, including responsiveness.
- Lead design strategy: uncover user and business needs, generate options, define an end-to-end product vision, and develop a roadmap.
- Empower and mentor designers while maintaining a collaborative, productive, positive environment.
- Communicate responsively with the team and company stakeholders while managing many concurrent projects.
- Partner with HR on a roadmap for learning opportunities that attract and educate talent.
- Collaborate with marketing to improve the company's digital presence.
- Ensure projects are delivered on time.
- Retain all Senior UI/UX Designer responsibilities.
- Stay current with UI/UX trends and design skills.

### Qualifications

- **Required:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- **Required experience:** 4+ years.
- **Required designation:** UI/UX Designer II or above.
- **Required evidence:** Strong team collaboration and participation in internal project communication and external client communication.
- **Required delivery experience:** Ability to manage multiple resources efficiently, with 3–4 years in UI/UX design, implementation, and client solution delivery.
- **Preferred:** UI/UX- or design-related degree or certification.
