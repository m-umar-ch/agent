# Night Work Compensation Policy

**Effective date:** July 17, 2026

## Definitions

- **Office Hours:** 10:00 AM–7:00 PM.
- **Night Hours:** 9:00 PM–2:00 AM.
- **Standby Duty:** A scheduled period during Night Hours when an employee must remain reachable and responsive, whether or not work is assigned.
- **Standby Duty Roster:** A project-specific schedule identifying which employees are on Standby Duty on each day.
- **Night Work:** Urgent bugs or issues, go-live support, and urgent features.
- **Eligible Projects:** Management selects projects based on client needs, workload, and previous data. The list is reviewed monthly.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** Project eligibility is revised monthly and is not listed in this handbook. A future agent must not classify a current project as Tier 1, Tier 2, or ineligible without checking the approved project list.

## Project tiers

**Tier 1** covers high-priority, strategically important projects where response time requires a standby roster, including:

- projects approaching launch;
- recently launched projects;
- high-usage or high-support projects; and
- projects requiring urgent features.

**Tier 2** covers medium-priority projects where a standby roster is described as unnecessary and team members are called as needed, including:

- launched projects with growing usage;
- projects in steady ongoing development; and
- projects with occasional support or bug-fix needs.

All other projects, including MVP development and enhancement work on stable launched projects, are ineligible for night-work benefits. Work on them should occur during Office Hours; qualifying extra work is handled only under the [overtime policy](./working-hours-and-attendance.md#overtime-and-rewards).

The source's benefits comparison states:

| Benefit | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Non-eligible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standby Allowance | Eligible | Not eligible | Not eligible |
| Night Work Pay | Eligible | Eligible | Not eligible |
| Overtime Pay | Eligible | Eligible | Eligible |

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source says Tier 2 does not need a standby roster and its benefits comparison grants Tier-2 employees Night Work Pay, but the Night Work Pay rule says payment applies only to employees on the Standby Roster. Future answers must state this contradiction and must not definitively say whether or how a Tier-2 employee qualifies until HR confirms the rule.

## Standby roster and allowance

- Team leads submit a monthly roster to the Head of Engineering (HOE) and HR.
- The roster identifies each employee's standby dates and response time.
- Only employees named on an approved roster qualify for the corresponding period.
- Each rostered member is expected to be available for 2 days and 3 days on alternate weeks.
- Standby Allowance is PKR 30,000 per month for each formally rostered employee.
- The allowance is paid even when no work is assigned during standby.
- Working late by personal choice or habit does not qualify.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source says each rostered member “must be available for 2 days and 3 days on alternate weeks,” but does not define which weeks carry two versus three days, how the rotation starts, or how partial weeks are handled. Future answers must quote the alternating two-day/three-day requirement and must not prescribe a specific schedule until HR confirms it.

## Night Work Pay

Night Work Pay applies to actual Night Work recorded in Hubstaff during Night Hours.

- **Standard hourly rate:** total monthly salary ÷ 160.
- **Night Work Pay rate:** standard hourly rate × 1.5.
- The compensation rule says Night Work Pay applies only to employees on the Standby Roster.
- The first 5 hours of "incidental Night Work" in a month are not compensated; the source says hours beyond that threshold are "paid in full."
- A rostered employee who actually works during standby receives both the Standby Allowance and Night Work Pay.
- The monthly cap is 30 Night Work hours per employee.
- A team lead must obtain approval for additional hours by email to HOE, with HR copied.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source says a monthly threshold of five hours of “incidental Night Work” is not compensated and that “hours beyond the threshold are paid in full.” It does not define incidental work or clarify whether crossing five hours makes all hours payable or only the hours above five. Future answers must state both claims and must not calculate a definitive payable-hour total until HR confirms the interpretation.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The policy is effective July 17, 2026, sets a PKR 30,000 monthly Standby Allowance and the rates above, and says rates are reviewed annually. Verify that the policy and rates remain current before calculating compensation.

## Next-working-day start adjustment

The source grants an extended adjustment to a rostered employee who performs actual Night Work on a Tier 1 or Tier 2 project: up to four hours on the next working day instead of the standard one-hour adjustment. Its example says an eligible employee may start as late as 2:00 PM rather than 12:00 PM.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source calls this an extension “instead of the standard 1-hour adjustment” and illustrates a move from 12:00 PM to as late as 2:00 PM, while also defining Office Hours as starting at 10:00 AM. It does not establish why 12:00 PM is the example baseline, and a four-hour adjustment from 10:00 AM also yields 2:00 PM. Future answers must state these source claims and must not definitively assign the baseline or exact start time until HR confirms it.

## Records, payment, and disputes

- Hubstaff records determine Night Work hours for the relevant pay period.
- The approved roster verifies Standby Duty.
- Raise a compensation discrepancy with HR within five days after payment.
- Standby Allowance and Night Work Pay are calculated monthly and paid with regular monthly salary.
- Rates are reviewed annually; changes are communicated in writing before taking effect.

## Exclusions and controls

The following are not compensated under this policy:

- personal breaks, non-work activity, or idle time tracked during Night Hours;
- feature development that can be moved to daytime hours; and
- Standby Duty or Night Work performed without prior assignment or roster approval.

Team leads must apply the policy fairly. Claims are subject to audit, and the company may revoke night-work benefits if it finds policy violations.
