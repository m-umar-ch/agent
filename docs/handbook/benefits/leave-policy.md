# Leave Policy

## Eligibility and annual quota

Full-time Staunch employees may apply for paid leave. Approval considers job performance, absenteeism, scheduling, and departmental requirements and remains at company discretion. Paid leave is separate from public holidays.

The standard annual paid-leave quota is 28 days:

- 8 annual-leave days;
- 10 casual-leave days; and
- 10 sick-leave days.

A maximum of three leave days per month applies except to annual leave. The source calculates leave on a fiscal-year basis, identified as July 1, 2024 through June 30, 2025. An employee joining after July 1 earns leave for that fiscal year pro rata.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source both grants a pro-rated fiscal-year quota to an employee who joins after July 1 and says annual leave becomes available only after one year of continuous service. Do not answer definitively that annual leave can—or cannot—be used in the first year. HR must confirm whether first-year accrual may be used, carried forward, or is limited to casual and sick leave.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The fiscal-year dates July 1, 2024 through June 30, 2025 are dated source values. Do not treat them as the current leave year until HR confirms the active fiscal-year cycle and quotas.

## Request and approval process

1. Submit every annual, sick, casual, or other leave request in Workable.
2. Workable routes the request to the employee’s team lead.
3. A request is not final until Workable issues a formal approval notification.
4. After approval, post the leave notification in the `#team-staunch` Slack channel.

General notice and attendance rules:

- Notify the team lead at least one day in advance.
- For emergency leave, notify the team lead as soon as possible and submit the Workable request within two days after returning.
- For late arrival or inability to report to work, notify the team lead before the scheduled start time.
- More than three consecutive days off without prior approval results in termination without pay under the source policy.
- A leave lasting more than three hours counts as a half-day and requires team-lead approval.
- Once the quota is exhausted, additional leave is salary-deducted; use the same request process.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** This section says more than three consecutive unapproved days results in termination without pay, while the offboarding policy treats absence for an unspecified number of days without notice as voluntary resignation. A future agent must state both claims and must not determine whether an absence is termination, resignation, or abandonment based on a specific threshold until HR confirms the governing rule.

## Sick leave

- Quota: 10 days per year.
- Permitted purposes: the employee’s illness or illness-related circumstances involving an immediate family member under the employee’s care.
- Notify the team lead as soon as possible, explain the absence, and provide an expected return date.
- For more than two consecutive sick days, provide medical documentation on return; otherwise, the leave is unpaid.

## Annual leave

- Quota: 8 paid days per year.
- The source states eligibility begins after one year of continuous service, subject to the first-year conflict noted above.
- Team-lead and HR approval is required at least 30 days in advance.
- Management or HR may deny a request because of excessive scheduling conflicts, peak production periods, or other operational hardship.

## Casual leave

- Quota: up to 10 days per year.
- Obtain team-lead approval in advance with HR copied; failure to notify in advance makes the leave unpaid.
- Provide a return-to-work date.

## Probation and notice period

- During probation, leave is allowed only in severe cases.
- Employees serving a notice period are not entitled to paid leave; leave taken then is unpaid.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The general employment section says every new hire has a three-month probation, while this leave section says probation may be 3 or 6 months. State both claims and do not give a definitive probation duration or probation-leave eligibility date until HR confirms the employee’s applicable term.

## Sandwich rule

The sandwich rule applies to all employees. When leave is taken immediately before and after a weekend or public holiday, the intervening weekend or holiday also counts as leave.

Example: leave on Friday and Monday causes Saturday and Sunday to count too, for a total of four leave days.

## Maternity leave

The source provides the following sequence for female employees:

1. 6 weeks of paid maternity leave.
2. Up to 1 additional month of unpaid maternity leave.
3. After that unpaid month, up to 1 month working from home, subject to discussion and approval by the team lead and HR.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source does not state whether the maternity work-from-home month is subject to the six-month eligibility rule or category schedule in the [work-from-home policy](./work-from-home-policy.md). Do not give a definitive answer beyond the required team-lead and HR discussion and approval.

## Paternity leave

- Eligibility stated in the source: male employees.
- Duration: 5 working days.
- Timing: within two weeks after the child’s birth.
- Approval: request and obtain team-lead approval in advance.

For maternity and paternity leave, notify the team lead and HR as early as possible and provide a birth certificate or relevant medical documentation.

## Public holidays

Employees are entitled to all public holidays declared by the Government of Pakistan. The source includes this undated reference list:

- Kashmir Day — February 5
- Pakistan Day — March 23
- Labor Day — May 1
- Youm-e-Takbeer — May 28
- Eid ul-Fitr — first, second, and third days, subject to moon sighting
- Eid ul-Azha — first, second, and third days, subject to moon sighting
- Independence Day — August 14
- Ashura — 9th and 10th Muharram, subject to moon sighting
- Milad un Nabi (S.A.W.) — subject to moon sighting
- Iqbal Day — November 9
- Christmas Day / Quaid Day — December 25

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The holiday list has no applicable year, and several dates depend on moon sighting and government declaration. Do not provide a definitive current holiday calendar from this list; verify the relevant year’s Government of Pakistan announcement and HR schedule.

## Leave encashment

- Unused leave may be encashed at the end of the fiscal year, up to 50% of the employee’s total leave quota for that fiscal year.
- Encashment is not available on dismissal, removal, or resignation.
- Payment is made with the second salary of the next fiscal year, identified in the source as August.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** August is a dated payment reference tied to the source fiscal-year example. Verify the active fiscal-year calendar and current payment month with HR before giving a payment date.
