# Workplace Conduct and Safety

## Dress Code

- Dress professionally and smartly.
- Do not wear clothing typical of workouts or outdoor activities, shorts, or clothing with offensive or inappropriate prints unless the company specifies otherwise.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source also prohibits "sleeveless shorts," which appears to be a malformed garment description. Confirm the intended restriction and who may approve an exception before relying on it.

## Professional Conduct

- Use appropriate language. Do not use rude comments, inappropriate words, or slang.
- Treat colleagues, clients, and stakeholders respectfully, regardless of grade, age, gender, or beliefs.
- Keep workplace discussions professional. Avoid personal, political, and religious debates.
- Keep workstations tidy and consume snacks and refreshments responsibly.
- Make personal calls only outside company premises.
- Report misbehavior to a manager or HR instead of engaging in a direct dispute.
- Management is available to mentor employees on personal issues.

## Safety Responsibilities

1. **Report hazards and injuries:** Immediately tell your manager about an injury, illness, safety hazard, or equipment issue.
2. **Use electricity safely:** Do not overload electrical outlets. Obtain approval before introducing new electrical equipment.
3. **Handle flammables carefully:** Use cleaning fluids and similar items with care.
4. **Lift safely:** Ask for help when lifting heavy items or moving furniture.
5. **Protect office property:** Do not remove office items without authorization. Report lost or stolen items to HR.

The [general employment policy](general-employment-policies.md#disciplinary-grounds) lists failure to follow safety rules as grounds for disciplinary action.

## Anti-Harassment

Staunch prohibits harassment of any kind, including verbal, visual, and sexual harassment.

- Managers must address and stop harassment immediately.
- Managers must enforce the policy through disciplinary action when needed.
- Employees should report misbehavior to a manager or HR.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source does not define a confidential reporting channel, investigation steps or timelines, protection against retaliation, or an appeal process. Employees should report concerns to a manager or HR, but a future agent must not invent procedural details.

## Smoking and Nicotine Products

The smoking rule covers tobacco products, e-cigarettes, vapes, and other nicotine-delivery systems. Use only the company's designated outdoor smoking areas.
