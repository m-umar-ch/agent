# Focus Hours Policy

## Schedule

Focus hours are **3:00 PM to 5:00 PM every day** and apply across all teams.

## Thursday No-Meeting Day

Thursday is a no-meeting day. Schedule all meetings outside Thursday and plan work to preserve the uninterrupted day.

## Slack Requirements

During focus hours:

- set a Slack status showing that you are in focus mode and the **3:00 PM to 5:00 PM** time window; and
- enable Do Not Disturb.

## Quiet Workspace

- Do not make loud phone calls or allow Slack tones or other notification sounds.
- Keep laptops, phones, tablets, and other devices on silent.
- Maintain the silent-device rule throughout the day, not only during focus hours.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source places the quiet-workspace rules under Focus Hours but explicitly says devices must remain silent throughout the day. A future agent may state that all-day requirement but must not extend every other focus-hours restriction beyond 3:00 PM–5:00 PM.

## Discussion Rules

- Do not hold non-technical or non-project discussions during focus hours.
- When a technical discussion is necessary, speak quietly or use text-based communication such as Slack.
- Do not tap colleagues, call across the room, or start casual conversations.
- For urgent communication, send a respectful Slack message and expect that the recipient may respond after focus hours.

## Compliance

Team leads and reporting managers reinforce this policy. Consistent breaches may be addressed in one-to-one meetings or team retrospectives.
