# Offboarding Policy

This policy applies when employment ends through resignation, layoff, contract completion, or discharge.

## Voluntary resignation

An employee must send a written resignation to both the direct manager and HR at least one month before the intended last working day. The notice must state:

- the reason for leaving; and
- the last working day.

HR coordinates the transition, including return of company property, review of post-termination benefits, clearance, and an exit interview.

## Absence without notice

The offboarding section says failure to report to work for a specified number of days without notice is treated as voluntary resignation, but it does not define that number.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The leave policy says taking more than three consecutive days off without prior approval results in termination without pay. The offboarding policy separately says failure to report for a “specified number of days” without notice is treated as voluntary resignation, but it does not specify that number. Future answers must state both source claims and must not definitively label an absence as abandonment, voluntary resignation, or termination based on a particular threshold until HR confirms the governing rule.

## Involuntary termination

### Discharge for cause

Employment may end immediately for misconduct or a severe policy violation, including:

- breach of the employment contract;
- fraud, embezzlement, or illegal activity against the company;
- discriminatory or harassing behavior;
- unlawful or immoral conduct at work;
- willful neglect of job responsibilities;
- intentional damage to company assets; or
- continued disregard of company policies.

The policy also states that, in misconduct or serious-misconduct cases, Staunch first attempts counseling or another appropriate action. If that does not resolve the matter, an investigation determines whether the allegation is substantiated. Confirmed misconduct may result in suspension with or without pay or termination.

### Discharge without cause

Employment may end without cause through a layoff or because of poor or unsatisfactory performance, including during probation.

### Review and notification

Before an involuntary discharge:

1. The reporting manager requests review by the Termination Review Board.
2. The board—CEO, department head, HR representative, and team lead—determines whether discharge is warranted.
3. If discharge is recommended, the HR representative and reporting manager notify the employee.

### Probationary termination

The company may terminate employment for unsatisfactory performance during probation, subject to the required notice.

> **Needs HR confirmation:** The source does not state the required notice period for probationary termination. A future agent must not provide a notice duration without checking the employee's contract and confirming the current rule with HR.

## Unsatisfactory-performance process

1. The reporting manager counsels the employee, sets clear improvement expectations, provides measurable improvement strategies, and identifies support resources.
2. The employee receives written notice stating the performance areas requiring improvement, the proposed review date, and available support resources.
3. The employee has seven working days after receiving the notice to respond.
4. If performance does not improve, the reporting manager sends HR a formal report describing the performance issues and prior attempts to resolve them, and also shares it with the employee.
5. HR and the reporting manager decide whether to take no further action, suspend with or without pay, or terminate employment.
6. Disciplinary action takes effect no earlier than five working days after written notice is given to the employee.

## Property, clearance, and exit interview

- Upon resignation, discharge, or termination, return all company property, including laptops, mobile phones, keys, and identification cards.
- Where permitted by law, unreturned property may result in deductions from the final paycheck.
- Every departing employee must attend an HR exit interview, normally during the final week of employment.
- HR completes a clearance process confirming return of company property.

## Rehire

An employee who leaves in good standing and gives proper notice may be eligible for rehire. A former employee must use the regular application process and meet all minimum role qualifications, including any required assessment.
